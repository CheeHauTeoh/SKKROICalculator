    Check your vocabulary

    Words that look the same on the lips (e.g. “pat”, “bat”, “mat”) are hard for people and machines alike. Pick phrases that look different, or use longer phrases.

    Hold mode is more accurate. Automatic mode can trigger on yawns, chewing or smiling. Higher = fewer wrong words typed, but more “not sure” prompts.
